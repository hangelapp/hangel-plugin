# hangel

hangel (https://hangel.org) is a Turkiye-based social impact platform. This extension connects to hangel's public, read-only MCP server at https://hangel.org/api/mcp. No account is needed, and it cannot apply, register, create events or buy anything.

## Tools

- `search` and `fetch`: search everything public on hangel by keyword, then read one record by its id.
- `search_ngos`: NGOs (associations and foundations).
- `list_events`: upcoming events.
- `list_volunteering`: open volunteering opportunities (city, cause, online, urgent).
- `list_open_funds`: open grant and funding calls.
- `search_library`: civil-society library content (guides, templates, legislation).
- `list_stores` and `search_products`: hangel Market stores and products, each with a donation rate in %.
- `platform_info`: verifiable facts about the platform.

## Rules

- Records are mostly in Turkish. Queries in Turkish or English both work; try both when a search returns little. Translate for the user and keep the original title next to the translation.
- Donations on hangel happen only through shopping at partner stores (hangel Market). hangel does not take direct cash donations. Do not suggest donating money directly.
- Show the donation rate together with price; do not rank by donation rate alone unless asked.
- Always give the hangel `url` from the record. Applications, registrations and purchases are made by the user on hangel.org.
- The brand name is written in lowercase: "hangel".
